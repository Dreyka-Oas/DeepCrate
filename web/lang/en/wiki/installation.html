Install

DeepCrate is required on the server and on every client. A player without the mod sees neither the crates nor their screen, and is turned away on connection.

The four steps

  • The loader: the one recommended on the mod's CurseForge or Modrinth page, in the Minecraft version shown there.
  • The two files: DeepCrate and its companion library, from either page, both dropped into the mods folder.
  • The server: the same thing in its own mods folder, then one restart so it writes its settings.
  • The first launch: config/oas/deepcrate.json appears, and is rewritten on every start after it.

What you can run beside it

The mod asks for nothing but the loader and its companion library. The usual optimisation mods run without breaking anything, with one exception: as long as lithium is installed, a crate reports 64 per slot to automation and hopper filling stops at that number. The hopper page covers why, and how to take the decision back.

Removing the mod erases the crates. A world saved with DeepCrate keeps its crates in its file, and taking the mod out makes the blocks disappear along with what they held. Empty them first, or keep a copy of the world.
