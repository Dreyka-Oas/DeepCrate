What the mod does

A chest slot holds 64. This one holds 512, and the whole crate follows.

The number sits on the crate rather than on a slot: a module dropped into the tab on the left edge of the screen lifts every slot at once, wherever inside it happens to be stored. The crate keeps the best offer rather than the sum, so a 256 module next to a 512 gives 512.

What goes on the tab.

The tab clipped to the left edge of the screen is not a fixed pair of slots but a column. The mod ships two cells, capacity on top and rows below it, and another mod can add more: the tab grows with them.

The hopper

A VANILLA HOPPER
STOPS AT 64

A hopper fills a container up to what an ordinary stack accepts and no further, whatever the target block can actually hold. The mod corrects that for its own crates, and it is the only part of DeepCrate that depends on what is installed beside it.
